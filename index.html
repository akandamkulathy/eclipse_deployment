Which defect and approach pairings work

A pairing is blocked when the 7 mm sheath cannot reach the defect without removing bone in this model, when the device would have to bend more than 40° to enter the defect, or when the approach does not reach that part of the disc at all. Blocked pairings cannot be selected above.

Layer stack, nucleus to nerve

  1. Dense seal layer at the inner wall, facing the nucleus. Woven PET.
  2. Parachute backing, the same woven PET carried out across the flange as one sheet, so there is no seam.
  3. Porous layer filling the defect against the cut annulus walls, where healing cells come from.
  4. Smooth outer retainer, flush on the outer annulus, facing the nerve root. ePTFE or dense PET.

Build a 5× bench prototype

Build steps are drawn for B and C. D needs a braided sleeve, which is not on the order list; expandable braided cable sleeving is the usual stand-in. A is kept as a reference only.
